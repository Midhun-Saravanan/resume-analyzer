What is ATS?

An Applicant Tracking System (ATS) is software used by companies to automatically scan, filter and rank resumes before a human ever sees them. Studies show that 75% of resumes are rejected by ATS before reaching a recruiter.

75%Resumes rejected by ATS
98%Fortune 500 use ATS
6 secAvg recruiter scan time
70%+Score needed to pass

✅ Do's

  • Use keywords from the job descriptionMirror the exact words used in the JD — ATS matches keywords literally.
  • Use a clean single-column layoutTwo-column resumes confuse most ATS parsers. Stick to one column.
  • Use standard section headingsUse "Work Experience", "Education", "Skills" — not creative names.
  • Save as PDF or DOCXBoth formats are ATS-friendly. Avoid image-based PDFs.
  • Include a Skills sectionA dedicated skills section dramatically improves ATS score.
  • Spell out acronyms onceWrite "Artificial Intelligence (AI)" so ATS catches both versions.
  • Quantify your achievements"Improved performance by 30%" scores better than vague statements.
  • Tailor your resume for each jobA generic resume scores low. Customize keywords for every application.
  • Include contact info at the topName, email, phone, LinkedIn, GitHub — all at the very top.
  • Use standard fontsArial, Calibri, Times New Roman — all safe choices for ATS.

❌ Don'ts

  • Don't use tables or text boxesATS can't read content inside tables or text boxes.
  • Don't use headers and footersMany ATS systems skip content in headers/footers entirely.
  • Don't submit an image-based PDFScanned resumes or image PDFs cannot be parsed by ATS at all.
  • Don't use fancy graphics or iconsVisual elements like charts and skill bars are invisible to ATS.
  • Don't keyword stuffListing keywords randomly triggers spam filters in modern ATS.
  • Don't use creative fontsDecorative fonts may render as garbled text in ATS.
  • Don't use photos on your resumePhotos are ignored by ATS and can cause parsing errors.
  • Don't use abbreviations onlyATS may not match "ML" with "Machine Learning" — spell it out first.
  • Don't use a functional resume formatChronological format is far more ATS-friendly.
  • Don't go beyond 2 pages if fresherFreshers should keep to 1 page for best ATS results.

Perfect Resume Section Order

ATS expects resumes in a specific order. Here's the optimal structure:

1
Contact InformationName, Email, Phone, LinkedIn, GitHub, City
2
Professional Summary2-3 lines targeting the specific role with key skills
3
Technical SkillsLanguages, frameworks, tools, databases — use exact JD keywords
4
Work Experience / InternshipsMost recent first, bullet points with quantified achievements
5
Projects2-4 relevant projects with tech stack and impact
6
EducationDegree, college, CGPA, graduation year
7
Certifications / AchievementsRelevant courses, hackathons, awards

Keyword Strategy

Keywords are the most important factor in ATS scoring. Here's how to use them effectively:

📋

Copy from JD

Use the exact same words from the job description — not synonyms.

🎯

Hard Skills First

Prioritize technical keywords — languages, tools, frameworks, certifications.

🔄

Use Variations

Include both "JavaScript" and "JS", both "Machine Learning" and "ML".

📍

Place Strategically

Put keywords in summary, skills AND experience bullets for max impact.

⚡

Action Verbs

Start bullets with — Built, Developed, Designed, Implemented, Optimized.

📊

Add Numbers

"Reduced load time by 40%" beats "improved performance" every time.
