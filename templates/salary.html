💡 How to Negotiate Higher Salary
01
Research before interview

Know the market range for your role and experience level going in.

02
Let them offer first

Always let the employer state the number first. Then negotiate upward.

03
Highlight premium skills

Skills like AWS, Kubernetes, and ML command 20-40% higher pay.

04
Consider full package

Factor in bonuses, stock options, remote work, and learning opportunities.

05
Show impact with numbers

Quantified achievements justify higher pay — "increased revenue by 30%".

06
Don't accept on the spot

Always ask for 24-48 hours to consider any offer — it's expected.
